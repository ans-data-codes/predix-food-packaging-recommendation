# Domain Knowledge Role

## Compatibility Rules

Future rule sets will encode feasibility constraints between food characteristics, storage conditions, and packaging options.

## Barrier Requirement Logic

Barrier requirements will be derived from validated commodity and condition-specific knowledge, not static hardcoded assumptions.

## MAP and Fresh-Produce Guidance

MAP suitability, breathable-film decisions, and gas-exchange guidance will be tied to verified commodity-specific sources.

## Shelf-Life Models

Shelf-life and degradation modeling will require validated empirical relationships and explicit assumptions.

## Expert Validation

PREDIX is a decision-support tool; final recommendations should be reviewed by packaging experts and validated through testing.
